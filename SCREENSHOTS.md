# DaemonCore GUI previews

These are design mockups showing the intended DaemonCore XFCE experience with
the real Core Horizon wallpaper and DaemonCore Dark palette. They are not
screenshots from a boot-tested ISO. Actual panel layout, fonts, icon rendering,
and application categories may change when the image is built and tested.
