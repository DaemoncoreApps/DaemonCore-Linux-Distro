DaemonCore Security Guidebook

Practical, offline guidance for defensive security and authorized testing.

Permission is the first technical requirement. Use these tools only on systems and networks you own or are explicitly authorized to assess. Record the owner, scope, dates, allowed techniques, and stop contact before beginning.

A repeatable workflow

  1. Define: write down scope, exclusions, time window, and stop conditions.
  2. Observe: collect low-impact facts before sending active probes.
  3. Validate: confirm findings with the least disruptive technique available.
  4. Record: preserve commands, timestamps, output, and relevant screenshots.
  5. Report: explain impact, evidence, and practical remediation.
  6. Restore: remove test accounts and data, then verify normal operation.
Commands in this guide use 127.0.0.1 or the documentation-only 192.0.2.0/24 network. Replace them only with an address that is inside your written scope.
